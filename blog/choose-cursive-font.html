Choosing type

How to Choose a Cursive Font for Names and Signatures

The most expressive option is not always the most useful one. Begin with the job your lettering needs to do.

Start with legibility

Enter the full name at the final approximate size. Look closely at unusual letter pairs and at the first and last letters. A font that looks charming in a sample may become hard to read in a real name.

Match the tone

Elegant scripts suit formal cards and invitations. A simple signature style feels personal but controlled. Handwriting styles are warmer and more casual. Let the message, not decoration alone, guide the choice.

Use contrast with care

Dark ink on a quiet light background is usually the safest starting point. If the result will be small, reduce flourishes and avoid very thin strokes.
