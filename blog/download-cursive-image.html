How to Download Cursive Text as an Image

A clean export makes it easy to place your lettering in a document, design, or presentation.

Prepare the preview

Write the final wording, select the font, and check every line break. Choose transparent canvas if the image will sit on a coloured or photographic background; choose white or warm if you want the background included.

Download a PNG

Select Download PNG in the studio. PNG is a good choice for most image editors, slides, and social graphics. Keep the original wording somewhere safe so that it can be corrected later.

When to export SVG

SVG keeps text editable but relies on the selected font being present when another device opens it. Use it for workflows that can manage fonts; use PNG when you need the appearance preserved.
