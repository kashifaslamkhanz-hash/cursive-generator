Cursive Fonts vs. Unicode Text: What’s the Difference?

Both can make words look special, but they travel through the web in completely different ways.

A cursive font is visual styling

A font tells a browser how to draw ordinary characters such as A, b, and 3. In the Flourish studio, your original text stays intact while the chosen script controls its appearance. When you export a PNG, that appearance becomes pixels—ideal for a graphic, invitation, or social image.

Unicode text uses different characters

Decorative copy-and-paste styles substitute mathematical or enclosed Unicode characters where they exist. They can work in a caption or profile, but coverage is incomplete and a device may display them differently. Punctuation and unsupported characters should remain unchanged.

Which should you use?

  • Choose a rendered cursive font for reliable visual artwork and downloads.
  • Choose Unicode for short text that needs to stay selectable and pasteable.
  • Keep a plain-text version whenever clarity or accessibility matters.
